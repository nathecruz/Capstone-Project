# Teacher Questionnaire: HabitAI Faculty Dashboard and App

## Purpose

This questionnaire gathers teachers' feedback about HabitAI, including the faculty-facing Admin Panel and, for teachers who have used it, Faculty mode in the HabitAI app. It focuses on usability, the usefulness and clarity of the information provided, support for teaching decisions, and privacy.

This is a project feedback instrument, not a clinical assessment or a validated psychological scale. Adapt it to your study protocol and obtain any required school or ethics approval before collecting responses.

## Participant information

You are invited to share your experience as a teacher or faculty member. Taking part is voluntary. You may skip any question or stop at any time. Do not include your name, a student's name, or other information that could identify a student in your responses. Report findings in aggregate and follow your institution's data-protection requirements.

By continuing, you indicate that you have read this information and agree to participate voluntarily.
